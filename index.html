CIS Hackathon 2026

FeedLens.

Train a simulated short-video feed and trace how a watch, like, dislike or skip changes what you see next. No sign-in, nothing to install — the recommendation engine runs in your browser.

Open the live prototype →

What you can do in it

  • Your Feed — watch, like, dislike or skip, and see your interest profile and the upcoming queue recompute from what you just did.
  • Behind the Feed — pick any candidate and see its score split into interest match, popularity, exploration and diversity, plus the exact action that earned its points.
  • Algorithm Lab — test whether 11.4 seconds of watching can outvote a dislike, how liking one topic entrenches it, and whether exploration breaks the loop.
  • Time Machine — change one past reaction, replay both histories, and compare where the ranking went differently.

The question underneath it

Can watching a post long enough raise your interest in its topic even after you tap Dislike? A dislike costs 4 points, a second of watch earns 0.35 — so past 11.4 seconds, it does. FeedLens computes that for you instead of telling you.

This repository holds the submission material: the brief, algorithm specification, code map and roadmap. The prototype source lives in yg-gms/feedlens-demo.
